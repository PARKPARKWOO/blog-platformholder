Holiday and travel prevention is not solved by one prescribed walk. It is mostly about who controls doors and how the pet moves between spaces.

## Separate the pet from visitor traffic

Korea's Ministry of Agriculture [advised preparing a separate space for pets during holiday visits](https://www.mafra.go.kr/home/5109/subview.do?enc=Zm5jdDF8QEB8JTJGYmJzJTJGaG9tZSUyRjc5MiUyRjU3Njk2MiUyRmFydGNsVmlldy5kbyUzRg%3D%3D) in its 2026 holiday guidance.

Assign one person to the entrance during arrivals and departures. Keep the pet in a familiar area away from that door, and tell visitors to alert the owner before opening it.

## Check the vehicle and destination in sequence

Before departure, inspect the carrier lock, leash and harness buckles, connection points, and wear. Check the connection before opening a vehicle door.

At the destination, inspect the entrance, windows, balcony, fence, and shared exits. Start in one secure room rather than releasing the pet into the full unfamiliar space.

## Carry identification and reporting information

[Article 15 of Korea's current Animal Protection Act](https://www.law.go.kr/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?lsJoLnkSeq=1028318041) covers registration and loss or registration-change reports for covered animals. Check both the visible tag contact and the phone number in the national registration system.

Keep current identifying photos, the accommodation address, and the destination district. The [Find-My-Pet website](https://findmypet.platformholder.site?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=fmp-holiday-safety-2026q3&utm_content=holiday-home) opens without installation; creating a missing post requires Kakao sign-in.

If a loss occurs, use the actual last-seen point at the destination and follow the [five-step lost-pet response checklist](/en/blog/find-my-pet/lost-pet-5-step-guide). For everyday identification, continue with [the new-owner tag, registration, and photo check](/en/blog/find-my-pet/beginner-pet-prep).

The useful pre-departure check is not a time promise. It is doors, carrier, unfamiliar space, identification, and reporting access.
