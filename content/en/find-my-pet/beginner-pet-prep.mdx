Lost-pet preparation is not about increasing anxiety. It is about checking whether everyday information can **connect a finder back to the owner**.

## Treat the ID tag and microchip as separate routes

A finder can read an ID tag. A scanner reads a microchip number and links it to registration data. The chip is not a GPS tracker.

[Article 15 of Korea's current Animal Protection Act](https://www.law.go.kr/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?lsJoLnkSeq=1028318041) covers registration, reporting a registered animal as lost, and reporting changed registration details. [Article 11 of the Enforcement Decree](https://www.law.go.kr/lsLinkCommonInfo.do?chrClsCd=010202&lsJoLnkSeq=1028781975) includes an owner's phone number among those details.

A [primary study of microchip data in Australian shelter admissions](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4494412/) examined how inaccurate registry information interfered with reaching owners. It did not measure Korea's registration process, but it shows why the chip and current contact information belong in the same check.

## Update photos when the appearance changes

Store original photos of the front, full side, and distinctive markings. Do not use a fixed monthly count. Retake them when coat length, body shape, collar, markings, or image quality no longer represents the pet.

A Find-My-Pet missing post accepts up to three supported image files. Choose three that show different identifying features.

## Confirm reporting and web access

Learn where the lost-pet report sits in Korea's national animal information system. Bookmark the [Find-My-Pet website](https://findmypet.platformholder.site?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=fmp-readiness-2026q3&utm_content=prep-home); there is no app to install.

Lists and detail pages are public. Creating a post and filing a sighting require Kakao sign-in. A filed sighting appears through the site's notification bell and notification page.

If a loss occurs, switch to the [five-step lost-pet response checklist](/en/blog/find-my-pet/lost-pet-5-step-guide). Before visitors or travel, continue with the [holiday checklist for doors and transport](/en/blog/find-my-pet/holiday-season-pet-safety).
