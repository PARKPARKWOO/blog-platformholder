The first hour matters, but not because reunion is guaranteed within it. There is no universal evidence for that promise.

Its value is simpler: **the last-seen facts are still clear enough to divide the work**.

## Fix one set of facts

Write these items once:

- last-seen time and map location
- direction of travel
- a photo showing the pet's current appearance
- collar, clothing, tag, and distinctive markings
- a contact method for reports

This note becomes the source for searchers, authorities, and every public post.

## Divide roles when help is available

With three people, keep one at the last-seen point, send one to check the area, and give one the publishing and reporting work. With two, combine the last-seen point and local check. Alone, publish and report the facts, then move and stop somewhere safe whenever you update them.

Do not use one behavior rule for both species. A [US household survey](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4494319/) found that recovery methods differed between dogs and cats. A [missing-cat study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5789300/) found different found distances with different outdoor histories. Neither study predicts every loss in Korea.

## Start three jobs together

1. Check the terrain around the last-seen point without skipping it.
2. Notify the national animal information system and the local government or shelter.
3. Share the same photo, time, place, and contact method in neighborhood channels.

Lists and detail pages are public; creating a post requires Kakao sign-in.

## Decisions that can wait

Do not lock in an unsupported search radius, reward amount, or flyer count before the facts are ready. When a new sighting arrives, update the time, place, and direction first.

Return to the [five-step lost-pet response checklist](/en/blog/find-my-pet/lost-pet-5-step-guide) for the full sequence. Then use [expanding the search without a universal radius](/en/blog/find-my-pet/how-far-do-lost-pets-travel) to plan the next area.

The first hour is not a promise. It is simply the earliest chance to get different people working from the same evidence.
