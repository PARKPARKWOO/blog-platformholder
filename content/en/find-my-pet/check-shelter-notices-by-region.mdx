Every night you open the same screen from scratch.

Pick the region, pick dog or cat, scroll dozens of photos, second-guess whether you already saw that one yesterday, hit next page. Half an hour later all you have is **"not today."** Tomorrow you start over.

## Why this particular task wears people down

Because it comes with a deadline.

Under Korea's Animal Protection Act, local governments publicly notice the animals they rescue, and if no owner is identified **within 10 days of the notice**, ownership transfers to the local government. An owner who identifies the animal before then can reclaim it. So for the first ten days, the check is something you cannot afford to skip — every single day.

And it isn't one screen. Animals don't follow administrative boundaries, so the district where they went missing isn't enough — **the bordering districts count too.** Three or four screens, nightly.

<Callout type="note" title="The goal isn't checking daily — it's not missing anything">
Half an hour of scrolling every night is a worse fit for the job than **being told when something new appears**. Optimise for a lower chance of missing a notice, not for a shorter check, and the routine becomes something you can sustain.
</Callout>

## We turned this into a region subscription

[Find-My-Pet](https://findmypet.platformholder.site?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=fmp-launch-2026q2&utm_content=notice-intro) syncs the Ministry of Agriculture, Food and Rural Affairs' Animal Protection Management System open data **every hour**. Closed notices drop off; only active ones remain.

On top of that, three things:

- **Region pages** — province to district, showing that area's notices together with the **shelters' phone numbers**. No login needed
- **Region alerts** — pick a region and species once, and new notices trigger an in-app notification
- **Combined search** — one query covering both missing posts and animals in shelter care

The point is that re-selecting the same filters every night stops being part of your day.

## Do this today

<Steps>
  <Step title="Step 1 — Open the district page for the loss area">
    On the [region notices page](https://findmypet.platformholder.site/abandonment/region?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=fmp-launch-2026q2&utm_content=notice-step1), pick a province, then a district.

    - You get that district's **notice list** and the **shelters' phone numbers** together
    - It opens without a login
    - The URL carries the region name, so it bookmarks cleanly

    Time: 1 min
  </Step>

  <Step title="Step 2 — Include the neighbouring districts">
    One district isn't enough. Open the bordering ones too.

    Rescue points and shelter jurisdictions don't always line up, so working outward **from the loss point** through two or three districts is the safer sweep.

    Time: 2 min
  </Step>

  <Step title="Step 3 — Call the shelter about anything close">
    Notice photos vary wildly with lighting and angle. **Don't decide from the photo alone.**

    Call the number on the notice and ask about:

    - Coat colour, size, sex, and any **distinctive markings** (spots, scars, tail shape)
    - A lookup by **animal registration number**, if you have one
    - Whether you can visit in person, and when

    Time: 5 min
  </Step>

  <Step title="Step 4 — Filter the home list by region and species">
    On the [home feed](https://findmypet.platformholder.site?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=fmp-launch-2026q2&utm_content=notice-step4), open the shelter-care list to see only animals currently in care.

    - Use the `all` / `dog` / `cat` tabs at the top
    - Then choose a **province** and a **district**
    - Clear it with the reset-region link

    Time: 1 min
  </Step>

  <Step title="Step 5 — Subscribe with 'get alerts for this region'">
    Once a province is selected, an alert button appears next to the filters. Press it while signed in with Kakao.

    - The subscription saves **exactly what's selected**: province only means the whole province, a district means that district, and the `dog` tab means dog notices only
    - For wide coverage, leave the district blank and subscribe at province level
    - While subscribed the button reads as active; press it again to unsubscribe
    - You can hold several subscriptions — one for the loss area and one for each neighbouring area works well

    Time: 1 min
  </Step>

  <Step title="Step 6 — Watch the bell">
    When a new notice appears in a subscribed region, it shows up on the **bell icon** at the top of the screen.

    - Refreshed every 30 seconds
    - Press the bell for the most recent alerts
    - The full history sits on the **notifications page**

    Time: 10 seconds, a few times a day
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="tip" title="One query, both sides">
Type a breed or a place name into the search box and you get **missing posts** and **animals in shelter care** in one result set, split into tabs. It's the quickest way to check both sides against the same criteria — and it works without a login.
</Callout>

## If you came here from the other side — looking to adopt

The same screen is also a list of **animals waiting for a family.**

Adoption procedures start after the notice period ends, so if one of them stays with you, it's worth telling the shelter you're interested ahead of time. Local governments vary: application forms, interviews and pre-adoption education are common, and some offer adoption fee or neutering support. A region alert means new arrivals don't slip past you.
