# Separate practical guidance from unavailable promises

Useful guidance is often mixed with a product promise that is not available when you try to follow it. The reader then has to work out what can actually be done today.

The platformholder blog reduces that ambiguity by checking practical guides against the current state of PocketFit, Mirror-View, Find-My-Pet, and Resell Ops. Availability differs by service, and the articles say so.

Each article follows the latest feature truth for its service. Only currently available functions are described in the present tense. When a product surface is not available, the post remains a knowledge guide; pre-launch coverage keeps the manual workaround and the scope of early conversations explicit.
