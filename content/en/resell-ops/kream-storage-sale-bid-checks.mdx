You may reopen KREAM's sell-bid screen even after deciding on a price. The problem may not be a mistyped price; it may be that **the screen context and time behind the decision were never recorded together**.

This article does not claim a level of competition or prescribe a bid-checking frequency. It covers only what current official guidance supports and a manual record you can use today.

## Price position can matter more than item count

Even for one item, writing down only your chosen sell-bid price removes context from the next decision. If other bids are added or changed, the relative position of your price can change, but official materials do not establish how often that happens or how competitive it is.

Rather than imposing a universal “times per day” rule, keep the observation time and the reason for the decision on the same row. This is not a formula that guarantees a match or profit. It is a manual checklist for avoiding context-free rechecks.

## What the official guidance confirms

KREAM's [official selling FAQ](https://kream.co.kr/faq?category=selling&list=true) describes a storage sale as sending an item in advance for inspection and warehouse storage, then placing a sell bid. After storage is complete, the seller can place a sell bid at a chosen price. The same guidance distinguishes an instant sale, which sells into the highest existing buy bid, from a sell bid at the seller's chosen ask.

The FAQ states a maximum storage period of 120 days. Extensions are in 30-day units and may incur fees, so recheck the actual expiration date and applicable costs on the item screen.

KREAM's [official seller-grade and fee FAQ](https://kream.co.kr/faq/221/) lists seller grades and fees, storage application costs, and warehouse fees under terms effective March 2, 2026. It also states that the policy may change. This article therefore does not copy a particular fee as a permanent value. When estimating proceeds, record the date and the official terms used, then recheck current guidance before deciding.

## Five fields for a manual record

| Field | What to record |
|---|---|
| product and size | the model and size that identify the actual selling unit, not only a product name |
| observed sell bid | the price you entered or are considering and the screen values used in that decision |
| observation time | date and time establishing when the price context was observed |
| estimated proceeds | an estimate using fee, storage-cost, and other assumptions checked on that date |
| storage deadline and fee checkpoint | the item's expiration date and the next date to recheck extension or fee terms |

Label estimated proceeds as an `estimate`. Actual settlement and costs can differ with transaction state and current policy, so do not treat the estimate as a confirmed value equivalent to the sell-bid price.

The seller chooses the review cadence. A record can be updated at **a real decision point**, such as when considering a price change or reviewing the storage deadline. This article provides no universal number of checks or bid-refresh interval.
